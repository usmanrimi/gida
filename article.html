Institutional GESI & Safeguarding Policy

A comprehensive zero-tolerance framework designed to protect vulnerable communities.

Policy Framework

GIDA enforces a zero-tolerance policy against gender-based discrimination, sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEAH), child abuse, human rights violations, and extractive development practices.

This framework is binding on all personnel, partners, and contractors. We believe that true prosperity can only be achieved when every community member feels safe, respected, and empowered.

Core Commitments

  • Absolute protection from exploitation.
  • Youth enterprise safeguards.
  • Dignified representation in all media.
