What "Where People Prosper" means

We measure prosperity across four connected dimensions.

GIDA defines prosperity holistically, as equitable economic wealth, physical and biological health, social dignity, and environmental resilience, working together rather than in isolation.

  • Economic prosperity, livelihoods

    Transitioning rural households, smallholders and off-farm NMSMEs from subsistence to profitable, market-led enterprises.

  • Health & biological well-being, One Health

    Ensuring access to bio-safe food systems, clean water, microbial soil health, and nutrition-sensitive value chains.

  • Climate & infrastructure resilience

    Constructing low-cost bioclimatic green processing hubs, clean-energy cold storage, and eco-friendly community spaces.

  • Social & gender equity

    Dismantling structural power imbalances so women, youth, and persons differently abled lead, govern, and thrive.

Message from Leadership

Building Resilient Rural Communities From Within

"Prosperity cannot be granted from outside a community; it has to be co-engineered, owned, and defended from within it. Our role at GIDA is to provide the systems, capital linkages, and tools to make that possible."

GIDA bridges rigorous science, low-carbon engineering, and market systems development directly to smallholders, youth, and women-led enterprises. By empowering local institutions and dismantling systemic barriers, we ensure rural transformation is permanent, dignified, and generational.

Executive Director / CEO
